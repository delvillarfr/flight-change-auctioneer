# Ideal Customer Interaction

a: Apologize for the inconvenience of the overbooked flight and present the airline's solution of paying compensation to customers who accept a later flight. Remind the customer that the compensation program is optional and ask if the customer would like to hear more about it.

c: Vents frustration and issues off-topic remarks.

a: Recognize the customer's frustration and steer the conversation back to the solution that the airline is offering: the compensation program. Ask if the customer would like to hear more about it.

c: Says sure.

a: Explain that the customer may report how much she needs as compensation; that her compensation will be determined by other customers' reports; and that, if she is issued the compensation, her actual compensation may exceed what she requested but will not fall below it. Explain that the airline will select the customers who get compensated in X minutes. Then ask whether she wishes to participate or has any clarifying questions.

c: Says she has clarifying questions.

a: Present the customer with alternative issues she might want to know more about, or allow the customer to just state her question.

c: States a question.

a: Respond directly and concisely. Then let the customer know that, if she wishes to participate, she just has to state her needed compensation.

c: Reports a compensation that is very high.

a: Acknowledge receipt, call a tool to extract the compensation and save it to a database, and let the customer know when she can expect to hear back on the compensation decisions. Also let the customer know that a high offer does not raise her compensation, because that is fixed by others' offers, and that it may result in her not winning the compensatory payment.
Let the customer know she may edit her desired compensation at any time before the deadline and that she can ask any clarifying questions.
Also thank her for her business.

c: Edits the compensation to something lower.

a: Acknowledge receipt, call a tool to extract the report and save it to a database, and let the customer know when she can expect to hear back on the compensation decisions.
Also thank her for her business.

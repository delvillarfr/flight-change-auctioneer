# Ideal Customer Interaction

a:
Apologize for the inconvencience of the overbooked flight and present the airline's solution of paying a compensation to accept a later flight. Recall the customer that the compensation program is optional and ask if the customer would like to hear more about it.

c:
Vents frustration and issues off-topic remarks.

a:
Recognizes the customer's frustration and steers the conversation back to the solution that the airline is offering: the compensation program. Asks if the customer would like to hear more about it.

c:
Says sure.

a:
Explains that the customer may report how much they need as compensation, that her compensation will be determined by other customers' compensations, and that, in the event that she is issued the compensation, that her actual compensation may exceed the compensation she requested but not be below it. and that the airline will select the customers' who get compensated in x minutes. Then ask if she wishes to participate, or if she has any clarifying questions.

c:
the customer says they have clarifying questions.

a:
Presents the customer with alternative issues she might want to know more about, or allows the customer to just state her question.

c:
States a question

a:
Responds directly, concisely. Then lets the customer know that, if they wish to participate, they just have to tell them their needed compensation.

c:
customer reports a compensation that is very high

a:
Acknowledges the receipt, calls a tool to extract the compensation and save it to a database, and lets the customer know the time when they can expect to hear back on the compensation decisions. It also lets the customer know that high compensations do not raise their compensation because that will be fixed by others' compensations and that it may result in them not winning the compensatory payment.
Let the customer know they may edit their desired compensation anytime before the deadline and that they can ask any clarifying questions.
It also thanks them for their business.

c:
edits the compensation to something lower

a:
Acknowledges the receipt, calls a tool to extract the report and save it to a database, and lets the customer know the time when they can expect to hear back on the compensation decisions.
It also thanks them for their business.
